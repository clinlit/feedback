# ClinLit AI feedback

Public issue tracker for [ClinLit AI](https://clinlit.com), an AI research assistant that answers questions about
clinical trials, drugs and the published literature from public data (ClinicalTrials.gov via AACT, and PubMed),
citing every claim back to its source record.

**[Open an issue](https://github.com/clinlit/feedback/issues/new/choose)** to:

- **Report a wrong or incomplete answer**: the most useful feedback. Tell us the question and what was wrong.
- **Report a bug** in the app or the site.
- **Report a data problem**: a trial or article that's missing, outdated or wrong.
- **Suggest a feature.**

Before opening one, search the [existing issues](https://github.com/clinlit/feedback/issues): someone may have
reported it already, and a 👍 on it helps us prioritise.

## Issues here are public

Anyone can read them. **Don't include personal health information** (yours or anyone else's), names, or anything
confidential to your organisation, such as unpublished research plans or the drugs and targets you're working on.

For anything private, including **privacy or security concerns**, email **hello@clinlit.com** instead.

## Good to know

- ClinLit AI is not medical, investment or regulatory advice.
- Its data comes from periodic snapshots of ClinicalTrials.gov and PubMed, so very recent changes may not be in it yet.
- The app's code isn't in this repository; this one is only for feedback.
